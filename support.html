Getting sounds in

What formats can I import?

Anything the system can decode: mp3, m4a, aac, wav, aiff, caf, and more. If the Files app can play it, Blurt can almost certainly take it.

How do I add a sound?

Tap Import Sounds on an empty board, or the import button in the toolbar. On Mac and iPad you can also drag files straight onto the board.

Can I record my own?

Yes — the microphone button in the toolbar. Recording goes straight onto a pad. On Apple Watch you can record on your wrist and send it to your iPhone.

Playing

What do the four playback modes do?

  • One shot — plays through to the end.
  • Hold — plays while you hold the pad, stops when you let go.
  • Toggle — tap to start, tap again to stop.
  • Loop — repeats until you stop it.

Hold a pad and open its settings to change the mode.

Nothing comes out when I tap a pad

Check the silent switch on an iPhone, and the volume slider in Blurt's own transport bar at the bottom. If a sound was moved or deleted outside the app, the pad will say the sound is missing — import it again.

Can I use a keyboard?

On Mac and iPad with a hardware keyboard, the number row and letter keys trigger pads. Each pad can be given its own key in its settings.

Effects

Where are they?

Hold any pad. You get EQ, reverb, delay, pitch, speed, distortion, compression, chorus, flanger and a noise gate, plus reverse, normalise, fades and trimming.

Do effects change my original file?

No. The file you imported is never modified. Effects are applied on playback, or baked into a cached copy, and you can always take them back off.

Syncing

How do I get my board on my other devices?

Settings → iCloud Drive. Your library moves there and your other devices pick it up shortly after. All of them need to be signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on.

Is it safe to switch?

Yes. Moving copies everything across first and only removes the original once the copy has succeeded, so an interrupted move leaves your library intact.

My other device has not caught up

iCloud can take a few minutes, and it will not sync at all on a low battery or a metered connection. Open the Files app and look in iCloud Drive → Blurt; if you can see the files there, the sync worked and the app will catch up.

Apple Watch

Why are only some sounds on my watch?

Space and transfer time. Mark the pads you want with Send to Apple Watch, and Blurt converts and transfers just those.

Do I need my iPhone nearby?

No. Once sounds have transferred, the watch plays them on its own.

Tips

What do I get for tipping?

Nothing, on purpose. Blurt is free, has no ads and locks nothing away. A tip is a thank you, and the app works identically whether or not you leave one.

I tipped and nothing happened

That is expected — the section header changes to say thank you and that is all. If you were charged and even that did not happen, write in and Apple can refund it.

Privacy

Blurt has no network code, no accounts and no analytics. It cannot send your data anywhere. The full explanation is in the privacy policy.

Something is broken

Email blurtboard.app@gmail.com with your device, your version of Blurt, and what you did just before it went wrong. Bugs get fixed faster than feature requests, but both are read.
